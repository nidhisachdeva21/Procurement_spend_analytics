STEP 4  ANALYSIS 3 - PRICE COMPARABILITY TEST (RESULT: NOT SUPPORTABLE)
==============================================================================
Tested whether the same item is bought at different unit prices across
departments (the standard maverick-spend test).

  62% of goods lines record Quantity = 1 with the full lot value
  in Unit Price - representing 77% of goods spend.
  Across 161 repeat-bought items, the median max/min unit-price ratio
  is 3,786x - e.g. 'bread' appears at $0.11 and $20,747.

  Unit-of-measure is not captured in this dataset vintage, so these ratios
  reflect inconsistent data entry (each vs case vs lot), not price variance.

  CONCLUSION: excluded from the savings model. Quantifying maverick spend
  would require PO-level data with unit-of-measure. Reporting a number here
  would have overstated savings by orders of magnitude.
